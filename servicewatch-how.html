How a review works

  1. Agree the scope

    Choose the domains and systems your team owns and approve the checks.

  2. Collect evidence

    Record which services respond and what they return at the time of the check.

  3. Choose an action

    Review the evidence with the system owner and decide what to keep, restrict or retest.

How findings get more context

Open a workflow to see what goes in, what the reviewer checks and what the team receives. All examples use fictional data.

ProposedBreach & threat intelligence

Bring breach mentions, ransomware claims and malicious-URL reports into a single review. A mention can justify investigation even when a compromise is unconfirmed.

  1. InputAn approved domain appears in a breach report.
  2. ReviewCheck the source, report date and exact domain. Look for independent corroboration.
  3. ResultA dated reference for the owner, with confidence and a decision on follow-up.
ProposedInternal segmentation & detection

A separately approved exercise would compare what the network should isolate with what its controls actually block or detect.

  1. InputTwo isolated lab zones and a written rule that they must stay separate.
  2. ReviewCompare the observed path with the intended boundary. Check whether an alert reached the monitoring team.
  3. ResultA map of observed paths and a report showing where to review a rule or an alert.
Tested prototypeReviewing exercise reports

The tested prototype imports selected findings from a synthetic exercise into the review workspace. The exercise itself takes place separately.

  1. InputA structured report from a fictional exercise.
  2. ReviewCheck origin and scope, then select which findings to retain.
  3. ResultSelected items enter review with their synthetic origin preserved.
Research onlySecurity data connectors

A curated directory helps identify candidate sources for IP reputation, vulnerability advisories and asset history. Each connector needs separate evaluation before use in the pilot.

  1. InputA reviewer needs context for a reported software version.
  2. ReviewIdentify a candidate advisory source. Check its permissions, coverage and update date.
  3. ResultA connector shortlist for technical evaluation.
ProposedContinuous monitoring

Scheduled rechecks would highlight newly exposed services and notify the team when a previously reviewed asset changes.

  1. InputA previously reviewed gateway and its last recorded state.
  2. ReviewRepeat the approved check and compare the two observations.
  3. ResultA change notice for the owner showing what appeared or disappeared.
