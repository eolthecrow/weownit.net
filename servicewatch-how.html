ServiceWatch

Your business uses websites and online services every day. ServiceWatch helps you understand what is visible from the internet, what may need attention, and which improvements to prioritize.

ServiceWatch is currently a controlled pilot. Assessments use approved targets and a restricted test environment; capabilities are still being validated.

Four simple steps

How ServiceWatch works

Think of it as checking the digital doors and windows of your business — and turning the observations into useful decisions.

01

Choose what to protect

We agree on the website or internet-facing systems to review and confirm the authorized scope.

02

See what's visible

Approved checks look at externally accessible services and relevant security settings.

03

Understand the findings

Observations become understandable findings, with supporting evidence and an explanation of what they may mean.

04

Decide what to fix

Your team can prioritize actions, record improvements and arrange a follow-up check where supported.

Illustrative example · Not a live customer finding

From a technical observation to a clear decision

A business may still have an old remote-access service visible on the internet, even though the team no longer uses it.

What ServiceWatch might observe

An externally reachable service

A remote-access service appears to be reachable from the internet. Exposure alone does not prove that a vulnerability exists.

Why it matters: If unnecessary or poorly secured, it could increase the opportunity for unauthorized access.

What the business can do

Review, improve, verify

Ask the IT team whether the service is still needed, check its protections and restrict or remove unnecessary access.

Next step: Use a follow-up check to confirm the change where supported.

What to expect

What the pilot offers today — and what's next

We distinguish current controlled-pilot activities from future product capabilities so you know what is and isn't included.

Current controlled pilot

Authorized external-exposure assessments, evidence collection, findings review and a customer workspace are being tested and refined. Participation and scope are agreed individually.

Planned next steps

Automated customer onboarding, scheduled continuous monitoring, change alerts and additional threat intelligence are development goals, not currently guaranteed pilot services.

A little more technical detail (optional)

Depending on the approved scope, ServiceWatch uses established discovery and security-check tools to observe ports, services, web behavior and security configuration. Results are kept with evidence and reviewed in context. This is not an automatic penetration test, and not every observation is a confirmed vulnerability.
