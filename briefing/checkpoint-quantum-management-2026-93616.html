What was reported

Official vendor / TAC guidance: Check Point's advisory directs affected Quantum Security Management deployments to sk1000171 and the corrected Jumbo Hotfix levels. Check Point reports limited exploitation and warns that LivePatch Takes 28/29 do not address CVE-2026-93616.

Applicability and impact

Check Point reported limited exploitation of a pre-authentication path-traversal flaw in Quantum Security Management that can lead to arbitrary script execution. This is an older but high-priority carry-forward item, not a new November discovery.

What to check and remediate

  1. Identify management servers and their R81/R82 Jumbo Hotfix Takes, exposed management web services, LivePatch levels and evidence of unexpected script execution.
  2. Apply official fixes according to Check Point sk1000171; do not assume LivePatch Take 28/29 covers this issue. Restrict exposed management access and investigate possible compromise.
  3. Compare exact installed hotfix Take with the corrected matrix, review management-server logs and integrity indicators, and verify SmartConsole/policy installation after patching.
