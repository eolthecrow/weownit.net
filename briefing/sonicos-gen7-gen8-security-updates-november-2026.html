SonicOS Gen7/Gen8 security updates address SSL-VPN authentication risk

SonicWall's November advisory covers multiple high and medium severity SonicOS vulnerabilities in Gen7 and Gen8 physical and virtual firewalls; the related CVE-2026-19868 SSL-VPN SAML/MFA bypass is reported as exploited, but requires checking the actual advisory conditions and alternative credentials.

What was reported

SonicWall's notice, published 3 November and updated 6 November, instructs Gen7 and Gen8 operators to prepare and install the security releases SonicOS 7.3.4 and 8.2.3, and consult the detailed PSIRT advisory SNWLID-2026-0014 for affected builds.

Applicability and impact

SonicWall's November advisory covers multiple high and medium severity SonicOS vulnerabilities in Gen7 and Gen8 physical and virtual firewalls; the related CVE-2026-19868 SSL-VPN SAML/MFA bypass is reported as exploited, but requires checking the actual advisory conditions and alternative credentials.

What to check and remediate

  1. Inventory SonicOS generation and exact build; identify SSL-VPN SAML configuration, alternative authentication paths, and unexpected VPN sessions.
  2. Apply the appropriate SonicOS 7.3.4 or 8.2.3 security release according to SonicWall's advisory and release matrix; restrict unnecessary VPN/authentication methods while arranging the upgrade.
  3. Record exact installed build on each HA peer, compare SSL-VPN and identity-provider logs, test legitimate MFA-backed VPN logins and HA failover.
