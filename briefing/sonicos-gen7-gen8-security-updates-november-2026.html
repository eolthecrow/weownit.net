SonicOS Gen7/Gen8 security updates address SSL-VPN authentication risk

CVE-2026-19868 affects Gen7/Gen8 SonicOS SSL-VPN when SAML login is enabled: someone holding valid credentials for an alternative login method may bypass SAML enforcement, including SAML-backed MFA. SonicWall investigated a report of potential exploitation; CISA added the CVE to its known-exploited catalog on 6 November 2026. Check exact affected builds and the PSIRT advisory.

What was reported

SonicWall's 3 November notice (updated 6 November) and PSIRT advisory SNWLID-2026-0014 identify fixed Gen7 7.3.4-7013+ and Gen8 8.2.3-8015+ builds. SonicWall reports investigating possible exploitation of CVE-2026-19868; Canada's Cyber Centre independently reports its CISA KEV inclusion on 6 November.

Applicability and impact

CVE-2026-19868 affects Gen7/Gen8 SonicOS SSL-VPN when SAML login is enabled: someone holding valid credentials for an alternative login method may bypass SAML enforcement, including SAML-backed MFA. SonicWall investigated a report of potential exploitation; CISA added the CVE to its known-exploited catalog on 6 November 2026. Check exact affected builds and the PSIRT advisory.

What to check and remediate

  1. Inventory SonicOS generation and exact build; identify SSL-VPN SAML configuration, alternative authentication paths, and unexpected VPN sessions.
  2. Patch affected firewalls to SonicOS Gen7 7.3.4-7013+ or Gen8 8.2.3-8015+, following the PSIRT matrix. SonicWall documents no vendor workaround; separately review unnecessary alternative authentication methods while planning the upgrade.
  3. Record exact installed build on each HA peer, compare SSL-VPN and identity-provider logs, test legitimate MFA-backed VPN logins and HA failover.
