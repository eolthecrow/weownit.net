FortiOS management HTTPS denial of service: CVE-2026-106174

FortiOS 8.0.0 and 7.6.4–7.6.6 have an improper-locking flaw that can let an unauthenticated attacker with network access disrupt the HTTPS management interface. This is an availability issue; public exploitation is not established by this advisory.

What was reported

Official vendor / TAC guidance: Fortinet PSIRT FG-IR-26-181 identifies FortiOS 8.0.0 and 7.6.4–7.6.6 as affected; upgrade to at least 8.0.1 or 7.6.7 on the corresponding train. The advisory concerns the HTTPS administration interface; it does not establish exploitation in the wild.

Applicability and impact

FortiOS 8.0.0 and 7.6.4–7.6.6 have an improper-locking flaw that can let an unauthenticated attacker with network access disrupt the HTTPS management interface. This is an availability issue; public exploitation is not established by this advisory.

What to check and remediate

  1. Inventory FortiOS versions and all interfaces exposing the admin GUI, including trusted-host and administrative access policies; check whether the management plane is internet reachable.
  2. Upgrade affected systems to FortiOS 8.0.1 or 7.6.7 or later as applicable; restrict management access to trusted administrative networks until verified fixed.
  3. Record version/HA status and configuration, confirm management reachability is limited to authorized sources, verify GUI stability and successful administrative login after patching.
