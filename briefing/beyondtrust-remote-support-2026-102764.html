BeyondTrust Remote Support: exploited origin-validation flaw (CVE-2026-102764)

CVE-2026-102764 allowed unauthorized file operations against affected Remote Support appliances. BeyondTrust and investigators report attacks before disclosure; patching alone cannot exclude a prior compromise.

weownit.net editorial analysis · weownit.net publication: 2026-11-03 · Reviewed: 2026-11-03

In focus

Vendor fix available · reported exploitation

What was reported

Official vendor / TAC guidance: BeyondTrust BT26-05 identifies improper origin validation in internal messaging, which may permit unauthorized appliance file operations. It is not described by the vendor as an authentication bypass. Remote Support SaaS was patched; self-hosted installations require patch verification or an upgrade.

Applicability and impact

CVE-2026-102764 allowed unauthorized file operations against affected Remote Support appliances. BeyondTrust and investigators report attacks before disclosure; patching alone cannot exclude a prior compromise.

What to check and remediate

  1. Locate Remote Support deployments, affected appliance builds, exposure, outbound connections and the access/system/security logs recommended by the vendor.
  2. Apply BeyondTrust BT26-05 fixes urgently and follow vendor/Mandiant incident-triage guidance; isolate systems with credible compromise indicators and rotate exposed secrets after investigation.
  3. Confirm patched builds and authorized connectivity, document reviewed logs/IOCs and forensic limitations, and verify no unexpected persistence or outbound tunnels remain.
