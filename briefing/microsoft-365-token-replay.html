What was reported

Huntress reports Microsoft 365 session replay and devices and MFA methods registered by attackers.

Applicability and impact

Check the tenant’s evidence before deciding which identities or sessions to restrict. Coordinate containment with the tenant administrator and business owner to preserve essential access.

What to check and remediate

  1. Preserve relevant logs and coordinate investigation with the tenant administrator.
  2. Review suspicious sessions, devices and authentication methods.
  3. Define post-response validation and monitor subsequent access attempts.
What to keep in mind

Choose actions from evidence and coordinate with the identity owner.
