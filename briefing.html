SonicOS Gen7/Gen8 security updates address SSL-VPN authentication risk

SonicWall investigated a report of potential exploitation; CISA added the CVE to its known-exploited catalog on 6 November 2026. Check exact affected builds and the PSIRT advisory.

What to examine
SonicWall PSIRT · SonicOS Gen7/Gen8

First action
Inventory SonicOS generation and exact build; identify SSL-VPN SAML configuration, alternative authentication paths, and unexpected VPN sessions.

Verify the fix

Record exact installed build on each HA peer, compare SSL-VPN and identity-provider logs, test legitimate MFA-backed VPN logins and HA failover.
