Privacy notice

Updated 19 November 2026

Vladimir Arjoca, based in Romania, is the controller for personal data processed through weownit.net. For questions or to exercise your rights, email contact@weownit.net.

Contact and consultations

To reply to your enquiry, the contact form requires your name, email address, message and agreement to use those details. Company and phone are optional. Cloudflare Email Service delivers the message and your selected language to my mailbox. Delivery logs record a request ID and outcome. I use your consent to answer general enquiries and take steps at your request when you ask about a contract.

Opening the booking calendar or a direct booking link connects you to Cal.com. If you book, Cal.com shares the contact details and time you choose with me so I can arrange the consultation you requested. I use the correspondence and booking details to manage the appointment and any follow-up you request.

Briefing emails

If you subscribe, Cloudflare Turnstile checks the signup for abuse. Cloudflare D1 stores your email, language, subscription status, consent version and relevant dates. Resend emails you a confirmation link valid for 48 hours. Once you confirm, Resend sends the Briefing in your chosen language on the basis of your consent.

You can withdraw consent using the unsubscribe link in any edition. The subscriber register keeps your email, opt-out status and date, and Resend suppresses further mailings to honour your choice. The expiry of a confirmation link does not erase a pending signup. You can also email me to request erasure.

Buying and downloading a toolkit

The store loads its catalog from checkout.weownit.net. When you buy a toolkit, you enter your email and payment details on Stripe’s checkout page. Stripe handles the card details. To fulfil your order, the checkout service uses Cloudflare D1 to record the product, quantity, currency, amount, Stripe transaction references, payment status and download access. Cloudflare R2 serves the purchased file.

Checkout sets an essential 24-hour cookie to recognise the browser entitled to the download. Clearing it can interrupt access. Where email recovery is offered, the service checks the address you enter against the Stripe order and uses Resend to send a single-use recovery link. Order records support delivery, refunds and disputes, and are retained as required for accounting and legal obligations.

Tools, the AI assistant and ServiceWatch

The browser-based firewall and incident evidence tools analyse imported files on your device. If you choose to include a review summary in an enquiry, this tab holds an editable draft valid for 20 minutes. Opening Contact removes the stored draft. You can edit the message before sending it. CVE Risk Lens sends the CVE identifier to a Cloudflare Worker. Opening an external Web-Check report shares the domain in the link with Web-Check. The public ServiceWatch guide and demo use fictional examples.

Assistant shortcuts run in your browser. For an AI answer, the browser sends your question, language and up to six recent chat messages to Cloudflare Workers AI. The site keeps the conversation in page memory and has no chat-history database. Use only information you can share. Leave out passwords, access keys and confidential client data.

Site visits and browser storage

GitHub Pages hosts the site and Cloudflare helps deliver and protect it. They process technical data such as IP addresses and request information. The site uses IP-based limits to prevent abuse. When you download the free sample, the site records the resource, language, country, referring hostname, source and time. I rely on legitimate interests to operate the optional tools, keep the site reliable, investigate failures and understand which samples visitors use.

Your browser saves your chosen language under siteLanguage until you clear the site’s data. The language also appears in page links.

Retention and international transfers

Retention depends on the purpose. For correspondence and bookings, the relevant period covers the request and its follow-up. For transaction records it also depends on accounting requirements and the time during which a claim can be brought. Technical logs support troubleshooting and abuse investigations. Browser cookies and confirmation links have the separate expiry periods set out above.

The providers listed below may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Depending on the provider and destination, safeguards may include an EU adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Their policies describe the arrangements. Email me for details or a copy of the safeguards relevant to your data.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, erasure or restriction of processing. Where applicable, you can request data portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal applies from that point onwards. Email me using the address above and identify the enquiry, booking, subscription or order involved. I normally respond within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with Romania’s ANSPDCP or the supervisory authority where you live, work or believe an infringement occurred.
