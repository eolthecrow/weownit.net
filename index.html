Consulting methodology

From findings to action.

A structured way to understand the environment, prioritize what matters, and implement improvements that can be validated and maintained.

01 / ASSESS

Understand the environment

Review architecture, exposure, controls, operational dependencies, evidence, and the current security posture before recommending change.

02 / PRIORITIZE

Focus on what matters

Translate findings into clear risks, priorities, owners, and practical remediation actions based on impact and feasibility.

03 / IMPROVE

Implement & validate

Strengthen controls, verify the outcome, document the change, and make improvements repeatable wherever possible.

What should a consulting review produce?

Clear findings supported by evidence, risk and impact context, an agreed priority, a named owner, a practical recommendation, and validation criteria.

How does the methodology apply to the resources?

The same cycle applies across incident response, security assessments, firewall hardening, segmentation, diagnostics, and automation: understand the current state, prioritize the gap, implement the change, and verify the result.
