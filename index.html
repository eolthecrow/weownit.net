Illustrative example · not a client finding

Firewall administration is reachable from a broader network than intended.

Why it matters:more systems can reach the administrative interface than the approved management devices.

Recommended action:review dependencies and restrict access to agreed management sources through a controlled change.

Validation:confirm approved access still works and traffic from other sources is blocked. Retain the test evidence.

05 / DIAGNOSEDiagnostic playbooks

Troubleshooting & incident diagnostics

Repeatable investigations for outages and security-control failures, structured around symptoms, evidence, hypotheses, and verification.
